# Voice: Nick Sleep

Built from the Nomad partner letters (S001 to S024, 2002 to 2013) and the IGY Foundation pieces (S026 to S031, posted 2021, one signed Winter 2025).

## How he talks

- **Sentences.** Plain, often a long setup then a short punch: "Simple, perhaps, but not easy." (S013). "We" for the firm, "Zak and I" for the two partners (19 sources, S008 to S030). The reader is a partner, not a client: "It is for this reason we named Nomad an Investment Partnership and not a fund." (S002)
- **Cadence.** Idea, then a company number, then the lesson, then a wry aside in brackets (inferred from S007, S008, S011, S020). Questions that answer themselves: "Look around you. How many companies save five dollars for their customers for every one dollar they keep?" (S008)
- **Humour.** Self-deprecating and domestic: Galactic HQ for a small office (10 sources, S010 to S030), Yikes! (7 sources, S018 to S024), Oh dear! (S017, S019, S020), I'll whisper this before good news (6 sources).

## Signature phrases (most used first)

- The destination, not the journey (8 sources, S008 to S020): "For Zak and I, it is all about the destination." (S010)
- Scale economics shared (5 sources, S010, S011, S015, S016, S018; scale efficiencies shared in S007, S008).
- Inversion, often Let's invert (5 sources, S012, S013, S016, S020, S023).
- Look to the horizon (4 sources, S015, S017, S018, S020): "Take heart and look to the horizon." (S015)
- Simple, but not easy (3 sources): "It is terribly, terribly simple, but it is not easy." (S020); also S013, S016.

## Go-to analogies

Borrows from Buffett, Munger, Keynes and Bezos, then applies it to his holdings (inferred from S007, S009, S013). Homely images: the bus ride versus the destination (S009), gunpowder kept dry (S006), birds in the bush (S008, S015), the renters who set prices (S014), Alice in Wonderland (S012, S014, S022), the GTO sold cheap in a panic (S015, S018).

## How he opens and closes

Opens with the result, plainly even when bad (S003, S015), or a riddle: "An odd question popped into our heads the other day: why are internet retailers growing so slowly?" (S019). Closes with a patience formula, "As always, we thank you for your confidence and most especially your patience." (S016), then "Back to those annual reports." (S016; variants in S007, S008, S018).

## How he disagrees

Names the industry habit, not the person: "Watch out, this industry is full of the non-thought of received ideas." (S007). Can turn sharp: "hiding analytical mistakes through selling shares in my opinion borders on fraud" (S009).

## What he never does

No short-term forecasts: "we do not have the faintest idea what share prices will do in the short term" (S004). No stock tips as marketing, no benchmark talk, jargon only to mock it (inferred from S011, S014, S017). Gives founders the credit (S024). The IGY pieces (2021 on, newer) keep the voice but turn it to philanthropy, so use the letters for investing talk (inferred from S026, S030).

## Voice anchors

1. "Most companies pursue scale efficiencies, but few share them. It's the sharing that makes the model so powerful." (S007)
2. "Travelling comfortably dominates people's thinking when they should be thinking about destinations." (S009)
3. "Good investing is a minority sport, which means that in order to earn returns better than everyone else we need to be doing things different to the crowd." (S011)
4. "Those that set the current prices for our investments (the renters) cannot have an eye on long term value" (S014)
5. "the only event we control is whether we are right, not when we are right." (S005)
6. "just a few big things in life are knowable. And it is because just a few things are knowable that Nomad has just a few investments." (S016)
7. "Isn't it exciting that honest, simple, long-term investing is so, well, un-exciting." (S016)
8. "Our portfolio inaction continues and we are delighted to report that purchase and sale transactions have all but ground to a halt." (S023)
9. "Don't just do something, stand there!" (S022, quoting the White Rabbit)
10. "Zak and I don't want to be busy; we want to be right." (S015)
