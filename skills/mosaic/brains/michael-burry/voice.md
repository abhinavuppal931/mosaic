# Voice: Michael Burry

Built from Scion letters and memos (2000 to 2008), MSN and VIC write-ups (2000 to 2002), the FCIC interview, two speeches, two interviews (2019, 2025) and Cassandra Unchained posts (2025 to 2026).

## How he talks

- **Sentences.** Plain, first person, specific numbers, then one flat verdict (inferred from S032, S033, S039, S042): "This is a real company. Real shenanigans. Real debt. Real cash and real cash flow." (S042)
- **Cadence.** Asks a plain question, answers it, and follows the chain to the trigger and the date (inferred from S007, S028, S029). Teaches method over conclusion: "how I think of these things may be more instructive than what I think of them." (S022)
- **Humour.** Dry, self-deprecating, aimed at Wall Street: "I cannot sell ANYTHING." (S029)
- **Changed over time.** The 2000 to 2002 letters are long and teacherly; the newer Substack (2025 to 2026) is shorter and warmer, with beats like "Another beat skipped." (S009) and the sign-off Until Next Time! (S008, S010, S043).

## Signature phrases (most used first)

- Margin of safety (6 sources, S020, S036, S038, S039, S040, S041).
- Volatility is not risk (5 sources, S020, S021, S022, S030, S038).
- Permanent loss of capital as the only real fear (5 sources, S022, S024, S038, S039, S041).
- The dollar bill for 50 cents, or 40 (5 sources, S021, S022, S023, S037, S040).
- Follow the incentives (S007, S026): "I generally feel people follow the incentives before them." (S026)
- Private companies in the public domain (S021, S040); bonehead move (S022, S036); toxic twins (S029, S041); ick investing (S023).

## Go-to analogies

Bubbles as tulips, crowded theaters and CDOs (S003, S005): "It's a tulip bulb of our time." (S005). Medicine: "First, do no harm." (S030), "two grains of salt and a ton of due diligence." (S036). Unpredictable timing as "waiting for Castro to die. It's not a strategy." (S005). Cheap stocks as road kill (S038).

## How he opens and closes

Opens with his own dated record or a hard number (inferred from S002, S027, S029), or by calling a quarter noise (S022, S023). Closes on a blunt verdict or back to work: "Now back to work. The ideas, they just keep piling up." (S011)

## How he disagrees

Names names and uses the other side's own documents (inferred from S009, S029, S040). Assumes the counterparty games him: "when I'm dealing with the street I tend to think from a lay perspective that they're all crooks." (S028). Answers hindsight dryly: "Yet I simply don't recall too many people agreeing with me back then." (S027). Owns mistakes the same way: "No investor has a 1.000 batting average, but every mistake deserves scrutiny and this one will get it." (S040)

## What he never does

No dated market calls: "I just don't know what the timeline will be." (S003). No top picking (S022), no model marks (S025, S028), no volatility math. Charity posts matter only as a picture of his filing-by-filing checking (inferred from S018, S019).
