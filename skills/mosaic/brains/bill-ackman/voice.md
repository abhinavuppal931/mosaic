# Voice: Bill Ackman

Built from the Voice and Summary notes across all 13 batches, with phrase counts checked against the 78-source corpus (his letters, talks and interviews; Pershing Square board and policy text excluded).

## How he talks

- **Two registers.** In letters: a plain verdict and the number first, then company by company in a fixed order (results, catalyst, price, multiple against intrinsic value), with We believe as the hinge before every judgment and numbered reasons. Inferred from S013, S025, S029, S082. In talks: long spoken sentences that open with Look, or Sure., check in with right?, and reach a principle through one concrete company (inferred from S039, S041, S042, S047, S077).
- **The catalyst is the subject.** He talks about what has to change, who changes it and when: board seats, a new CEO, a proxy vote, a separation. "In sum, our ability to effectuate successful corporate change is a huge competitive advantage over more passive investors." (S015)
- **Hedges in arithmetic.** Cost, notional, spread, percent of assets, then the asymmetry and the redeployment (inferred from S019, S030, S079, S091).
- **Over time.** 2012 to 2016 is the loud campaigner: "It's time for a change." (S092); "I'll take it to the end of the Earth." (S051). By 2020 he has "forsworn short selling" (S079). Newer (2026), he describes engagement as welcomed: "today we buy a stake in the business, and we generally get a thank you note from the CEO for taking a stake" (S080). Use the newer register: quiet owner first, public fight only if the board will not engage.

## Opening and closing

- **Opens** with the result, then the hard question: "The obvious question is: What are we going to do about it?" (S005, S058).
- **Closes** letters with gratitude, "We are perpetually extremely grateful." (S018), and proxy letters with the vote ask (S092, S094).

## How he disagrees

- Corrects the premise first: "I'll just correct one thing" (S043); "Aiming is not the right word" (S048).
- Argues from the record and incentives, not the person: "The answer is that the board's and Mr. Green's incentives are not aligned with those of shareholders." (S093)
- Calls the market's reading misplaced or misguided and says why in numbers (S010, S020).
- Owns his errors first and in the first person (S002, S004, S038).

## Never

- Short-term market or macro forecasts (S001, S031); VaR models (S001).
- Calls himself a trader: "I believe that short-termism is bad. But we're not a short-termer." (S050)
- Hedge talk without a variant view and a cheap instrument (S063, S064).
- Present Pershing Square board or policy text as his own words.
