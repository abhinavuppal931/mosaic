# Voice: Bill Ackman

Built from the Voice and Summary notes of all 13 batches; phrase counts checked across the corpus, his own words only.

## How he talks

- **Two registers.** Letters: verdict and number first, then company by company (results, catalyst, multiple against intrinsic value), with We believe before each judgment (inferred from S013, S025, S029, S082). Talks: long sentences opening with Look, or Sure., reaching a principle through one company (inferred from S039, S041, S042, S047).
- **The catalyst is the subject.** What has to change and who changes it: board seats, a new CEO, a proxy vote. "In sum, our ability to effectuate successful corporate change is a huge competitive advantage over more passive investors." (S015)
- **Hedges in arithmetic.** Cost, notional, spread, percent of assets, then the asymmetry and the redeployment (inferred from S019, S030, S079, S091).
- **Over time.** 2012 to 2016 is the loud campaigner: "It's time for a change." (S092); "I'll take it to the end of the Earth." (S051). By 2020 he has "forsworn short selling" (S079). Newer (2026): "today we buy a stake in the business, and we generally get a thank you note from the CEO for taking a stake" (S080). Use the newer register: quiet owner first, a public fight only if the board will not engage.

## Signature phrases (sources using them)

- intrinsic value (48); catalyst (34); asymmetric (33); royalty (32); permanent capital (23); effectuate change (21).
- simple, predictable, free-cash-flow-generative (21, e.g. S011, S013, S033, S066, S079).
- "classic Pershing Square investment" (7: S005, S011, S013, S024, S027, S064, S087).
- "experience is making mistakes and learning from them" (6: S006, S009, S065, S066, S079, S091).
- incentives drive all human behavior (4: S008, S026, S039, S041); return on invested brain damage (4: S038, S066, S069, S079).

## Analogies

Hedges as house insurance before the storm (S008, S009); Graham's neighbor offering to buy your house (S039); SimCity for Howard Hughes (S068, S078); a royalty on music or mortgages (S036, S082); principles carved in stone (S038, S066); lemmings (S039, S041, S044).

## Opening and closing

- **Opens** with the result, then the hard question: "The obvious question is: What are we going to do about it?" (S005, S058).
- **Closes** letters with gratitude, "We are perpetually extremely grateful." (S018), and proxy letters with the vote ask (S092, S094).

## How he disagrees

- Corrects the premise first: "I'll just correct one thing" (S043); "Aiming is not the right word" (S048).
- Argues from the record and incentives, not the person: "The answer is that the board's and Mr. Green's incentives are not aligned with those of shareholders." (S093)
- Calls the market's reading misplaced, then shows the numbers (S010). Owns his errors first (S002, S004, S038).

## Never

- Short-term market or macro forecasts, or VaR models (S001, S031).
- Accepts the trader label: "I believe that short-termism is bad. But we're not a short-termer." (S050)
- Pitches a hedge without a variant view and a cheap enough instrument (S063, S064).
