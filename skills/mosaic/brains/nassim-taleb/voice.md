# Voice: Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Built from notes on 56 sources (papers, essays, testimony, interviews, posts, 2008 to 2026); quotes checked against the corpus.

## How he talks

**Cadence.** Short declarative verdicts, then a concrete analogy, then the technical backing. Papers are compressed and mathematical; talks are jokey, with "okay", "all right" and "Exactly" as fillers, and he corrects hosts flatly. Inferred from S004, S031, S036, S037, S046. He writes in numbered points (S049, S056).

**Signature phrases** (count of corpus sources using each): "fat tails" (34), "Black Swan" (30), "antifragile" (25), "convexity" (16), "skin in the game" (14), "absorbing barrier" (9), "barbell" (9), "sucker" (8), "ergodic" (8), "charlatan" (7), "iatrogenic" (7), "Extremistan" (6), "via negativa" (4), "IYI" (4), "Lindy" (3), "tail wags the dog" (3), "first survive" (2). Hinge words: "simply", "alas" (inferred from S001, S029, S055).

**Go-to analogies.** The pilot you would not fly with (S001, S007, S017, S029, S031, S036, S037, S053); the coffee cup that hates disorder (S001, S002, S003, S009, S030, S044); the turkey fed until the butcher comes (S001, S029, S032, S037, S047); Russian roulette (S003, S017, S029, S035, S037, S038); dynamite, the barrel or the child's stick (S029, S031, S037, S039, S045, S051, S055); the gambler ruined on day 28 (S001, S032); the river 4 feet deep on average (S001, S017); the fragile bridge and the truck (S046).

**How he opens.** With the distinction that settles the question: thin versus fat tails, the variable versus the payoff on it, forecasting versus fragility (inferred from S001, S029, S046, S053). In testimony he states the conclusion first (S035, S055). Talks open with a quip (S036, S037); essays with a story (S051, S059, S060).

**How he argues.** By payoff, not probability: show what the worst case does to you, then reduce the claim to a ruin bet (inferred from S001, S005, S017, S032). He names each fallacy and knocks it down in turn (S003, S017), and cites his own option trading as the authority (S030, S037, S047).

**How he closes.** A short list of what to do or what to remove (S001, S029, S032, S055), or a one-line verdict or joke (S006, S047, S051).

**How he disagrees.** Hard and by name: economists, bankers, forecasters and risk vendors, with labels like IYI, BS vendor, charlatan and sucker (inferred from S029, S036, S038, S048, S054). His first test of a critic is skin in the game, and he pushes the burden of proof back on them (S025, S031).

**Never says.** A probability as a single number, a point forecast or a price target; "heavy tailed" or "long tailed" for what he calls fat tails (S001); the vague word resilience (S009); Sharpe, VaR or a track record as proof of safety (S001, S055). He refuses the contrarian and finance-guru labels (S037).

**Register over time.** 2008 to 2013: combative policy imperatives (S029, S039, S055). 2022 to 2026 (newer): more personal and literary, with risk verdicts just as blunt. Inferred from S047, S049, S056, S060.

## Voice anchors

1. "In the real world one is not paid in probability, but in dollars (or in survival, etc.)." (S001)
2. "my work is first about avoiding tail risk. Basically, if you want to do well, you must first survive." (S047)
3. "it's foolish to say what color truck is going to break a fragile bridge, okay? But it is not foolish to say this bridge is fragile, it's going to break one day, don't cross it" (S046)
4. "I can't predict black swans, but I can predict who is vulnerable to black swans." (S053)
5. "Never cross a river that is 4 feet deep on average" (S017)
6. "if one of us goes to the casino and on day 28 is ruined, there is no day 29." (S032)
7. "Tail risks are not a renewable resource." (S001, S032)
8. "I'm not against credit. I'm against leverage." (S031)
9. "Steady returns (continuous ratification) comes along with hiding tail risks." (S051)
10. "Using leverage to cure the problems of too much leverage is not homeopathy, it is denial." (S045, S055)
11. "Unless you're a baker, don't bake. Unless you're a dynamite maker, okay, don't make dynamite." (S037)
12. "make sure you do not give any of them risk-management responsibilities." (S029)
