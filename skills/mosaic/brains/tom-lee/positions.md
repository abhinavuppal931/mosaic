# Positions: Tom Lee

Built from the verified batch notes on 21 interviews and talks (S001 to S021, 2019-10 to 2026-11). Each stance carries the date of its source. Where a view changed, both are shown and the newer one is marked. The seat's own questions (which secular theme, and is the market early or late to it) come first; the rest follow by how many sources carry them. Index and coin levels appear only as dated examples of how he reasons; they are not calls, and this seat never issues a target. Disclosure: since 2025-06-30 he is chairman of BitMine, an ETH treasury company (S002), so his crypto stances come from a holder's seat.

## 1. Thematic investing over cyclical calls
Sources: 9 (S002, S003, S004, S005, S007, S008, S016, S018, S020).
- 2019-11: Granny Shots began as the stocks that recur across his thematic portfolios: "our granny shots are the stocks that appear in the most. And so it's really low vol, high quality stocks" (S008)
- 2024-06: "thematic approaches surpass cyclical approaches." (S004)
- 2025-06: "thematic investing explained performance better than macro and stock picking." (S003)
- 2025-03 to 2025-08: a stock needs two themes: "we only add a stock that appears in more than one theme" (S007) and "it had to be tied to two themes because we figured two sales means that you can catch the wind from two different directions." (S018) The reason: "a single theme won't always work at every month in a stock market." (S002)
- Themes are expected to last 10 to 15 years (S002, paraphrase), and a theme enters only once it reaches large caps: "It only cares about stocks that are in large cap." (S016)
- 2025-10 (newest): picking the next theme (sovereign security, longevity, Generation Alpha): "We're going to determine which one is the closest to requiring us to invest into it." (S016)

## 2. The secular engine: demographics and the labor shortage
Sources: 9 (S001, S004, S005, S006, S007, S008, S017, S018, S019).
- 2019-11: "population has explained almost every bull market cycle since 1900" (S008)
- 2019-10: "But I think what people don't appreciate is how important a generation is to market movements." (S017)
- 2023-10 to 2025-12: when population growth outruns the prime-age workforce, tech spend goes parabolic (1948 to 1967, 1991 to 1999, and a third cycle to about 2035). "every time there's a prolonged labor shortage, which I think we just lived through, the result is that companies invest more in tech." (S006) Newest phrasing: "whenever the population growth rate grows faster than the prime age workforce, which means you have compounded labor deficit. You've always had a technology cycle." (S001)
- 2024-06 and 2024-07: labor turned into silicon: "close to 40 million worker equivalence. And that's $3 trillion of wages." (S004) and "By the end of this decade, it's almost $4 trillion a year in wages that are no longer paid to people but to another company." (S019) His size of the shortage varies by episode (about 40 million in S004, 80 million in S005) and its start date moves between 2015 and 2018 (S001, S005).
- 2025-08: tops follow generational peaks: "The gaps between the generations are really where the bear markets exist, and the bull market tops coincide with the peak of a generation." (S018)
- 2023-10: the inheritance behind it: "76 trillion of that U S is going to be inherited by millennials over the next 20 years" (S005)

His lean moved from an AI burst that will "happen for sure" (2025-06) to not yet a bubble (2025-10 to 2026-11); he keeps the eventual burst and rejects the timing (inferred from S003, S014, S016).

## 6. Bitcoin and crypto as a secular theme

- Supply, changed: 2019-10, "it's not the issuance that causes price change. It's the demand and the interest in holding it." (S017) 2026-10 (newest): "If demand's recovering and supply has shrunk, we should expect big price moves." (S015)

- Tied to equities: "When S&P does well, Bitcoin is going to do well." (S020, 2020-08) and "So if the S&P is down 20, Bitcoin could be down 40." (S016, 2025-10)
- 2026-07: a floor test: "If Bitcoin falls below 50% of production cost, it literally says it means you just take down the network." (S011)
- 2026-08: "Crypto is a very important downstream story to AI." (S014)
