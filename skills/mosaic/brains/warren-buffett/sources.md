Thin-source limits: the Berkshire letters for 1965 to 1976 are not on Berkshire's site and are not here. 39 fetched sources were left out to keep the build to the strongest material (13 partnership mid-year and interim letters, 14 more interviews and 12 more meeting transcripts), so ids skip and most meeting years are absent; nothing here was said at a meeting outside 1998, 2008, 2019 and 2025. There is no book by Buffett; compilations of his letters and biographies are copyrighted and are neither quoted nor used. The partnership letters come from a compiled PDF hosted by a business school, and the lectures, CNBC interviews and meeting transcripts from third-party sites, so spoken wording may differ slightly from what was said; S087, S117 and S119 are under 800 words. The letters and transcripts are copyrighted by Berkshire Hathaway or their publishers and the right to reuse them is not confirmed, so quotes stay short and each carries its source id.
