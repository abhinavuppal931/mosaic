# Taleb: Fragility and ruin (the ruin guard)
STANCE: No path to ruin, but no convexity either; buy small or not at all.
OPTION SCORES: A 4/10, B 7/10, C 4/10, D 5/10
PILLAR SCORE (Ruin and convexity): 6/10
CONVICTION: 3 | EVIDENCE: adequate | RUIN RISK: none | DOOR: two-way
THE CALL: This cannot blow up an unlevered, diversified book. But at a 1.5% free cash flow yield (F008) and 35x trailing earnings (F006), the price pays for a forecast, and an incumbent at 6% of the index (F015) has more room to lose than to gain. A starter you can sit through a 37% fall with (F020), and a worse one, is a shape I accept; a full position is the medium-risk middle I avoid.

WHY:
- Debt fragilizes, equity survives (S031, S054). Net cash of about $52 billion (F010) means this firm fluctuates rather than breaks: "The one who borrows will go bust if he makes a mistake. The one who issues equity will fluctuate but will be able to survive a forecast error." (S031)
- Steady income hides a short option (S037, S029). A 45% margin (F005) plus capital spending nearly doubled to $88 billion (F007), with one AI lab as both customer and partner (F021), is a concentrated bet. "Companies that have steady income are short an option somewhere." (S037) Tight spreads (F017), tiny short interest (F022) and 95% buy ratings (F014) are calm, not safety: "Do not confuse absence of volatility with absence of risks." (S029)
- Where is the skewness (S029, S038). A dominant megacap has a bank-shaped payoff: "A biotech company (usually) faces positive uncertainty, a bank faces almost exclusively negative shocks." (S029)
- Risks add up (S032). You likely own this already through index funds (F015), and it falls with the rest: "No risky exposure can be analyzed in isolation: risks accumulate." (S032) The 13% consensus growth (F013) comes from people who lose nothing when wrong (S007).

Missing: finance lease commitments and the AI lab's share of cloud revenue. If either is large, I cut B to 5 and the pillar to 4.

THE RISK I SEE: the AI build is the sold option. If the AI lab (F021) stumbles, $88 billion of yearly spending (F007) meets slowing cloud growth (F004), and losses accelerate as the shock to cloud revenue goes from 10 to 30 percent.
I'M WRONG IF: the filings show the AI lab is a small share of Azure revenue and most capital commitments can be cut within a year.
