# Damodaran: Valuation
STANCE: Great company, priced as great; I wait for a price that needs only a probable story.
OPTION SCORES: A 3/10, B 5/10, C 7/10, D 4/10
PILLAR SCORE (Value vs price): 4/10
CONVICTION: 3 | EVIDENCE: adequate | RUIN RISK: none | DOOR: two-way
THE CALL: "I have neither a crystal ball nor the monopoly on the truth." (S005) Still, I would not buy a full position at $500 (F001): the business is superb, but the price assumes a decade near today's growth, margins intact through its biggest build. I would set a limit order and wait (C); a starter (B) is a fairly priced holding, not a bargain.
WHY:
1. Read the price backwards (Breakeven revenue, S087). I assume the missing cost of capital at 8% to 10% and tax near 20%; change them and the answer moves. Holding the 45% margin (F005) and 30% returns (F011), $3.7 trillion (F002) needs revenue of roughly $0.9 to $1.5 trillion in ten years, 12% to 18% a year off $290 billion (F003), far past the $400 billion only five companies had reached in January 2023 (S071). Put simply, it needs consensus (F013) or better for a decade.
2. The 3P test (S034). With cloud up 35% (F004) that path is plausible, not yet probable, and I "treat the possible as icing on the cake (great if it happens, but you are not paying for it up front)" (S012). Here you pay for the icing.
3. Clean the cash flows (Accounting cleanup, S044): "adding back any expense that will persist as part of regular operations is bad practice" (S044). Net of $12 billion stock compensation (F009), $57 billion of free cash flow (F008) yields near 1.2%, against a 4.2% Treasury (F016). Capital spending has nearly doubled (F007); "AI has built the most expensive factory in history, and done so in hyper speed" (S094); its return is the number I cannot see yet.
4. Good company versus good investment (S085): "most great companies are neutral or even bad investments, because the market prices them to be great" (S085). With 95% buy ratings (F014), the market agrees already.
THE RISK I SEE: I have sold compounders too early (S084); if the new capacity earns 30% like the rest, my distribution is too narrow. The AI lab concentration (F021) cuts the other way. Time will tell.
I'M WRONG IF: revenue grows 15% or more (F003) for the next eight quarters with the operating margin holding at 45% (F005) as the new capacity depreciates.
